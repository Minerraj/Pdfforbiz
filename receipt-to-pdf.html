Where your files go: nowhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side PDF processing — no upload, no storage, no account.

How it works

  1. Photograph the receipt with your phone.
  2. Upload one or more photos here — each becomes its own page.
  3. Click Create PDF — download a single, properly filed PDF.

Who uses this

  • Filing expense receipts for reimbursement or tax records
  • Bundling several receipts from a business trip into one document
  • Keeping a digital paper trail without a dedicated scanner app

Can I add more than one receipt at a time?

Yes — select or drag in multiple photos and they'll each become a separate page in the same PDF, in the order added.

What image formats work?

JPG and PNG, which covers standard phone camera photos and screenshots.
