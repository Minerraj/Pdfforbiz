Is a drawn signature legally binding?

In most jurisdictions, a simple electronic signature like this is legally valid for everyday business documents. For contracts with strict legal requirements, check whether your situation needs a certified e-signature service instead.

Can I place the signature on any page?

Yes — use the page navigation to go to the page you need, then click where you want the signature placed.
