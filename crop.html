How it works

  1. Upload the PDF you want to crop.
  2. Drag on the page preview to draw the area you want to keep.
  3. Click Crop & download — every page is trimmed to that same area.

Who uses this

  • Trimming wide margins off a scanned document before sharing
  • Removing a printer's crop marks or border from a PDF
  • Cutting a PDF down to just the content area for a cleaner presentation

Does cropping apply to every page or just one?

The crop area you draw is applied to every page in the file, using the same position and size.

Does cropping delete the content outside the box?

No — it changes the visible page boundary, which is how most PDF crop tools work. The original content still exists in the file but won't display or print outside the cropped area.
