PDFforBiz vs Smallpdf vs iLovePDF

Where PDFforBiz fits best

If your PDF work is mostly invoices, receipts, contracts, and client paperwork, and you'd rather not upload financial documents to a third-party server, PDFforBiz is built specifically for that. It's narrower by design — 10 tools instead of 20+ — but every one of them runs entirely in your browser, so files never leave your device.

Where Smallpdf or iLovePDF fit better

Both are more mature products with a much wider tool set — OCR, e-signatures, form filling, and dozens of format conversions PDFforBiz doesn't offer. If you need one of those specific advanced features, they're the better choice.

The core difference: where processing happens

Smallpdf and iLovePDF process files on their servers, which is standard for most online PDF tools and generally safe for typical documents. PDFforBiz processes everything locally in your browser instead — a meaningful difference if you're handling sensitive financial documents like invoices with account numbers or client contracts, since nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Bottom line

For the everyday small business tasks — merging invoices, compressing files for email, stamping "PAID" on an invoice, redacting an account number — PDFforBiz covers it for free with a privacy-first approach. For advanced or less common needs, Smallpdf and iLovePDF's broader toolsets are worth having bookmarked too.
