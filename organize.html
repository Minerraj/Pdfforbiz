Organize PDF

Reorder or delete pages in a PDF.

See every page as a thumbnail, reorder or remove what you don't need, then download. Nothing leaves your browser.

Useful when a scanner puts pages out of order, or a merged document has a blank page that shouldn't be there. A free alternative to Adobe Acrobat's page organizer, with no subscription required.

Organize PDF

Upload a PDF to see every page as a thumbnail you can reorder or delete.

Click to choose a file or drag a PDF here
Where your files go: nowhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side PDF processing — no upload, no storage, no account.

How it works

  1. Upload the PDF you want to reorganize.
  2. Use the up/down arrows on each thumbnail to reorder pages, or the remove button to delete one.
  3. Click Save & download once the order looks right.

Who uses this

  • Fixing a scanned contract where the pages came out of order
  • Removing a blank or duplicate page from a merged document
  • Reordering a proposal so the pricing page comes last

Can I remove a blank or unwanted page?

Yes — click the remove button on any thumbnail to delete that page before downloading.

How do I fix a PDF with pages in the wrong order?

Upload the file here, use the up/down arrows on each page thumbnail to reorder them, and download the corrected version.
