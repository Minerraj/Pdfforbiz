Split PDF

Pull specific pages out of a PDF.

Choose a page range to extract into its own file — handy for pulling one invoice out of a bundled statement. Nothing leaves your browser.

Bank and vendor statements often bundle several invoices or transactions into one long PDF. This tool lets you pull out just the pages you need without touching the rest of the file. Works the same as paid PDF splitter tools, without the subscription or software install.

Split PDF

Upload a PDF, then enter the page range you want extracted (e.g. "2-4" or "1,3,5").

Click to choose a file or drag a PDF here
    Where your files go: nowhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side PDF processing — no upload, no storage, no account.

    How it works

    1. Upload the PDF you want to pull pages out of.
    2. Type the page range you need — a single page, a range like "2-4", or a list like "1,3,5".
    3. Click Extract — just those pages download as a new PDF, in their original order.

    Who uses this

    • Pulling one invoice out of a bank or vendor statement that bundles several together
    • Separating a signed contract page from a larger document
    • Extracting a single client's records from a shared report

    What page format should I use?

    Either a range like "2-5", a list like "1,3,7", or a mix like "1,3-5". Page numbers start at 1.

    Do the extracted pages keep their original order?

    Yes — pages are extracted in the order they appear in the source file, regardless of the order you type them.

    How do I pull one invoice out of a bank statement PDF?

    Upload the statement, type the page number(s) the invoice appears on (e.g. "4" or "4-5"), and extract just those pages into their own file.
