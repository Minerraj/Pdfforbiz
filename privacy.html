Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PDFforBiz ("we," "our," "the site") provides free PDF tools for small business paperwork. This page explains what happens to your data when you use them.

Your files

Every tool on this site — Merge, Compress, PDF to Word, Receipt to PDF, and Redact — runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, stored, or seen by us in any form. When you close the tab, the files are gone.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall site traffic — for example, which pages are visited and roughly how many people use the site. This tracks anonymized usage patterns, not the contents of any file you process.

Advertising

This site may display ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on general browsing activity. You can learn more or opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings.

Cookies

We do not use cookies to store or track anything related to the files you process. Any cookies present come from third-party services (analytics or advertising) described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on our About page.
