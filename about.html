About PDFforBiz

PDFforBiz is a small set of free PDF tools built specifically for the paperwork small business owners deal with every month — invoices, receipts, statements, and client documents.

Most PDF tool sites are built for general use. PDFforBiz is narrower on purpose: every tool here is designed around the workflows small businesses and freelancers actually run into — merging invoices for tax season, compressing files for bookkeeping software uploads, turning a photographed receipt into a filed PDF, or redacting account numbers before sharing a statement.

How it works

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded to a server, stored, or seen by anyone — including us. Once you close the tab, everything is gone.
