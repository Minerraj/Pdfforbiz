Watermark / Stamp PDF

Stamp "PAID" across an invoice.

Add a diagonal text watermark — PAID, DRAFT, COPY, or your own text — to every page. Nothing leaves your browser.

A quick visual stamp saves confusion when a client or bookkeeper is sorting through a folder of invoices — mark what's paid, what's a draft, or what's a duplicate copy at a glance. A free alternative to paid PDF stamping tools — no subscription, no install.

Watermark PDF

Upload a PDF, choose your stamp text and color, then apply it to every page.

Click to choose a file or drag a PDF here
    Where your files go: nowhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side PDF processing — no upload, no storage, no account.

    How it works

    1. Upload the PDF you want to stamp.
    2. Type your stamp text (PAID, DRAFT, COPY, or anything custom) and pick a color.
    3. Click Apply — the stamp is added diagonally across every page, then the file downloads.

    Who uses this

    • Marking an invoice as PAID before archiving it
    • Labeling a contract DRAFT before it's finalized and signed
    • Marking a duplicate COPY sent to a second recipient

    Can I use custom text instead of "PAID"?

    Yes — clear the text field and type anything up to 20 characters, like "DRAFT," "COPY," or a company name.

    Is the watermark removable later?

    The watermark is drawn into the page content, similar to how most stamping tools work — it's meant to be a permanent visual marker, not a removable overlay.

    How do I stamp PAID on an invoice PDF for free?

    Upload the invoice here, leave the stamp text as "PAID" (or change it), and apply — it's added diagonally across every page instantly.
