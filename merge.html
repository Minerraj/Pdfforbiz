Merge PDF

Combine invoices into one clean PDF.

Stack multiple invoices, receipts, or statements into a single file — reorder as you go. Nothing leaves your browser.

Bookkeepers and small business owners often need to merge several invoice PDFs into one file before filing for tax season or sending to an accountant. This tool combines them locally on your device, so nothing is uploaded anywhere. No need to buy Adobe Acrobat or install anything — this works the same on a laptop or phone.

Merge PDF

Add your files in the order you want them stacked, then combine into one PDF.

Click to choose files or drag PDFs here
    Where your files go: nowhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side PDF processing — no upload, no storage, no account.

    How it works

    1. Add two or more PDF files — invoices, receipts, or statements — by clicking or dragging them in.
    2. Reorder them using the up/down arrows until they're stacked the way you want.
    3. Click Merge — the combined file downloads immediately as one PDF.

    Who uses this

    • Bookkeepers combining monthly invoices before sending to an accountant
    • Freelancers merging multiple receipts into one file for expense reports
    • Anyone bundling contracts, appendices, or attachments before sending to a client
    After this, you might also want to: Compress PDF PDF to Word Redact PDF — or read how to organize invoices for tax season and how to merge bank statement PDFs.

    Can I change the order of the files before merging?

    Yes — use the up and down arrows next to each file to reorder them before merging.

    Is there a file size or page limit?

    No hard limit is enforced, since everything runs on your device rather than a server. Very large files depend on your device's available memory.

    How do I merge PDF files without Adobe Acrobat?

    Just use this page — add your files, reorder them if needed, and click Merge. No Acrobat, no install, and no account required.
