    Why choose PNG instead of JPG?

    PNG is lossless, so text and sharp lines (like a logo or diagram) stay crisp. JPG produces smaller files but can slightly blur fine detail — better for photo-like pages.

    What if my PDF has multiple pages?

    Each page becomes its own PNG, packaged together in a single zip file for download.
