Compress PDF

Shrink a PDF so it fits email limits.

Reduce file size for invoices, statements, and reports before emailing or uploading. Nothing leaves your browser.

Most email providers cap attachments around 25 MB, and accounting platforms like QuickBooks and Xero have their own upload limits. This tool cleans up bloated PDF structure locally to bring the file size down before you send it. No Adobe Acrobat subscription needed — this handles the same job for free, right in your browser.

Compress PDF

Works best on files with a lot of embedded fonts or repeated content — image-heavy scans will see smaller gains.

Click to choose a file or drag a PDF here
    Where your files go: nowhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser using client-side PDF processing — no upload, no storage, no account.

    How it works

    1. Upload the PDF you need to shrink.
    2. Click Compress — the tool cleans up bloated fonts, formatting, and unused data.
    3. Download the smaller file, ready for email or upload.

    Who uses this

    • Getting an invoice under a 25MB email attachment limit
    • Meeting file-size requirements when uploading to QuickBooks, Xero, or a client portal
    • Keeping a shared drive or inbox from filling up with oversized PDFs

    Why didn't my file get much smaller?

    This tool cleans up document structure — duplicate fonts, unused objects, formatting bloat. Scanned or image-heavy PDFs are dominated by image data, which this method doesn't recompress, so gains will be smaller on those files.

    Will compressing reduce quality?

    No — this method doesn't touch page content or image resolution, so what you see stays exactly the same.
